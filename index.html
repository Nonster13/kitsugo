N5 GRAMMAR

は — the topic particle

は marks what a sentence is about. It is written は but pronounced wa when used as this particle.

[topic] は [information]
わたしは学生です。Watashi wa gakusei desu. · I am a student.
これは本です。Kore wa hon desu. · This is a book.
